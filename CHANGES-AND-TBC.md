# EVO website: changes made and open items

For EVO, as asked for in section 10 of the brief.

## Changes made

**Site-wide**
- New positioning throughout: a fully managed, fixed-price repairs service for housing landlords. "Digitally Transforming Property Management", "#HassleFree" and "a fusion of..." removed (GLOBAL-04).
- Bronze, Silver and Gold plans, all old prices, annual prices, "15p a day", Heating Protect and Home Protect removed everywhere. Plans teaser linking to /pricing in their place (GLOBAL-01).
- All prices say "per home per month, plus VAT" (GLOBAL-02).
- "23% missing compliance checks", "35% and 60%" / "35-60%" savings and the block reporting price removed (GLOBAL-03).
- Product names: EVO Living App, EVO Trades App, EVO Dashboard (GLOBAL-05).
- Awards: Housing Executive Awards 2025 shown only against IDS. Housing Digital Innovation Awards 2024 shown only against B&D Reside, including the hero and footer (GLOBAL-06).
- Plan coverage: up to 85%, 95% and 99% (GLOBAL-07).
- Brand: EVO orange for signals, navy for structure, Quicksand font, plan cards all one neutral colour (GLOBAL-08).
- Real EVO photos only, captioned in small grey type (GLOBAL-09).
- Book a portfolio review, Talk about a 12-month pilot and Book a demo all go to the contact form with the enquiry type pre-selected (GLOBAL-10).
- New navigation in the order set out in section 4. Contact is a button labelled "Book a portfolio review".
- Banner now promotes Awaab's Law phase 2 and switches wording automatically on 30 November 2026.
- Footer: Plans & pricing, Damp & mould, Case studies and Residents links added; award labels corrected; frameworks and accreditations updated; company number added.
- Storm logo removed. No competitor names or logos anywhere.
- Organization, FAQPage and HowTo structured data. Page titles and meta descriptions on every page. Sitemap and robots.txt.
- 301 redirects for moved URLs.

**Pages**
- Homepage rebuilt in the HOME-01 to HOME-14 order. The seven private landlord testimonials moved to /sectors/landlords. "UK housing sector is in crisis" copy moved to /about/why-we-do-it and softened. Explainer video moved to /how-it-works.
- /pricing: native plan explorer with all three organisation types, Managed Technology, three plans with "What's included" drawers, both compliance add-ons, a sticky price summary that passes the selection to the contact form, what sits outside the plans, footnotes, FAQs and an at-a-glance price table. Slideshow typos fixed ("Plumbing", "Locksmith", "service", ligatures), stray "£500 is plus VAT" footnote dropped, duplicate "heating pipework up to 10 metres" listed once.
- /damp-and-mould: new, all eight blocks, plus the one-page PDF.
- /case-studies, /case-studies/ids, /case-studies/bd-reside: new.
- /sectors/housing-associations and /sectors/local-authorities: rewritten (HA leads with IDS, LA with B&D Reside). /sectors/build-to-rent leads with Home Trust and Build to Rent prices. Landlords and letting agents kept, old pricing and savings claims removed.
- /how-it-works: new, replaces /about/how-we-do-it, including the comparison table by category.
- /residents hub, /how-to-guides and the two guides rebuilt as web pages (PDFs kept as downloads).
- /faqs: resident FAQs updated (damp and mould question added); landlord FAQs now "Landlords and property managers" with the pricing FAQs and no plan-tier references.
- /about/trust: new. /contact: new "What would you like?" field, new sector options, "How you found EVO" optional, hidden plan-explorer fields, Mark Iandoli's contact block.
- /products pages: aligned to the How it works copy, "#HassleFree" and protection plans removed, "12-month warranty on every job".
- Insights: "Damp and mould" tag added. Article cover images added.
- New /renters-rights-guide mailing list sign-up page (replaces /download-sign-up, which redirects), linked from the landlords page.
- Resident registration form linked from the resident FAQs and the residents hub.
